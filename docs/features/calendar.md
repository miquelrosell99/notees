# Calendar and day pages

A date is a node, not a string (SCHEMA.md "Dates"), and the day page is the date's home in the UI. Two surfaces render it — the Calendar's day view (a dedicated aggregation surface) and the day page itself (an ordinary page, so it also appears in the journal feed, search, and everywhere else pages do) — and both read the *same* materialized state, never a bespoke query. This page covers both surfaces, quick capture, and the date model; the tasks that surface on days are [tasks.md](tasks.md)'s; the date property's picker is [properties.md](properties.md)'s.

## The Calendar

The sidebar's **Calendar** entry (hide it from Workspace Settings → Sidebar Visibility) opens the calendar — **grid-first**: the month grid with the month's **event blocks**. A **Day / Week / Month / Year** ladder in the top bar switches the zoom (the choice is device-local): **Day** is the day view described below; **Week** is the selected day's week as seven day-columns with its blocks; **Month** is the grid (neighbor-month days render dimmed, "+N more" caps a cell and jumps to the day); **Year** is twelve compact months with event dots — a month name jumps to that month. Blocks wear the event's **effective color** (its own, else its first class's); a block click opens the event, a **day number opens the day page**, clicking a cell selects the day.

**Drag-and-drop (the Google register)**: drag a block onto another day to move it — plain events rewrite their date; a **recurring** event asks which events to move — **Only this event** (detaches it, like every calendar app), **This and following events** (the series splits), or **All events**. Press-drag across empty cells creates an `event`-classed object spanning the dragged days (a multi-day drag stamps the date value's `endDate` metadata — the span covers every day in the range, one block per day; the value keeps pointing at its start day). Right-click a block for Open / Delete — deleting a recurring event asks the same three questions.

- **Top bar** — a **Today ‹ [Month] [Year] ›** navigator centered in the bar: the chevrons step the visible period by the zoom's step (a week in Week, a month in Month, a year in Year; the period also follows the day you pick in the grid), and the month/year are the panel/popup title buttons — clicking opens the monthly/yearly date-chain pages. The Day zoom's context shows the weekday, a Today marker, the full date in your date-format setting, and the ISO week number. (The old ‹ / › day-stepping arrows retired — day picking rides the grid.)
- **The grid is the agenda** — the event blocks ARE the events list (owner 2026-10-24, the redundancy ruling): the system `event` class and everything extending it (Meeting, Birthday, your subclasses). An event blocks a day when **either** its date property points at that day (a multi-day `endDate` span blocks every day of the range), **or** it lives as a block at any level inside that day's page. A node that merely carries a date property without being event-family stays in Dated, not here. Block labels ride the title register: an inline class chip resolves to the class's current name; membership-only events read "Untitled". The right column keeps only the **mini-month navigator** (click a day to select it, the month/year titles open the date-chain pages) — the Google/Apple sidebar register. The events LIST itself (the zoomable, grouped, breadcrumbed agenda) lives in the **Journal's side panel**: the journal is the day-page context that agenda belongs to, and picking a day there scroll-navigates the feed to that day page (creating it if missing).
- **Daily note** — the selected day's page embedded inline as an **editable outline**: its child blocks render through the shared collection outline (edit titles and text where they render, drag to reorder, the trailing "+ Add block" row appends at the end) — no page header, the top bar already names the day. No page yet? The "+ Daily Note" button creates it in place.
- **Repeating events** — an event (or task) whose date carries a **repeat** rule shows on every occurrence day: the Dated and Tasks sections list it (one row per series — occurrences are computed, not stored, so there are no copies to clean up), and the month grid dots every occurrence day. Editing the event edits the whole series — unless you act on ONE occurrence (the calendar's edit/delete on a recurring block): the app asks the Google register — **Only this event** (the occurrence edits into a detached ordinary event; a deletion just removes that day), **This and following events** (the series splits: everything before the day keeps the old series, the day onward carries your edit as a new series), or **All events** (the whole series). The exception state rides the date value's metadata (`exdates` / `until`) — syncs like everything else, no hidden objects. Set or change the rule from the event's **Properties** panel (the ⟳ picker on the date pill — re-picking the date keeps the rule), or clear it back to "Does not repeat". The picker offers None / Daily / Weekly / Weekdays (Mon–Fri) / Monthly / Yearly. A repeating task appears in Scheduled on each occurrence day and never goes Overdue — the series rolls forward instead. **Completing one occurrence completes only that day**: the occurrence-done toggle (the tasks hub's checkbox on the day context, the task's own surfaces) records the date (the occurrence renders struck-through, every other day stays open, reopening removes just that date) — the tasks hub's checkbox, off the day context, still means the whole task (the checkbox semantics are [tasks.md](tasks.md)'s).
- **Daily note** — the selected day's page embedded inline (edit it where it renders; an embedded feed entry skips the page chrome per the embedded-feed rulings). No page yet? The "+ Daily Note" button creates it in place.
- **Tasks** — open tasks scheduled for the day as plain **editable outline rows** (the shared collection grammar — inline title/text editing, drag to reorder; no checkbox, status pill, or repeat marker, owner 2026-10-23), with overdue ones grouped above under their own heading. "New" creates a task scheduled for the selected day. A task appears here when its **Scheduled** property points at this day; Done and Cancelled tasks never list.
- **Dated** — everything else that references this day through a date property (a meeting held that day, a range ending on it), plain editable outline rows riding the **breadcrumb trail** above each row (its containing-page ancestry — the collection default).
- **Created** — objects created on the selected day, newest first, plain editable outline rows with breadcrumbs, same as Dated. Main objects only — blocks created that day are inline scaffolding, not listed.
- **Month grid** (right column) — the selected month with today highlighted, days that have a note dotted, and prev/next arrows; click any day to select it. The **month and year title are clickable buttons** exactly like the calendar popup's (the month prominent, the year greyed — clicking opens the monthly/yearly date-chain pages); the Days/Months/Years switch zooms the grid (jumping back to today rides the top bar's Today button). A day some object **references** through a date property (a meeting held that day, a range end) is dotted too — not just days that already have a note. Calendar breadth rides the edge index: date refs and range ends fan out to the deterministic day node at projection time, so "objects dated this day" is one materialized backlink read — the month grid's range-aware dots (a day with dated objects but no note still shows) derive from it, per the same exclusion rules as the Dated section. (Qualified-link metadata ranges edge from their start day only — the middle days stay undotted, the honest visibility of the current model.) Under the grid, the **events list** rides the same materialized reads plus a subtree walk over the scope day pages ([above](#the-calendar)).
- **Reviewed days** — a day page carries a **Reviewed** boolean property (set it from the day page's Properties). Reviewed is a property, not a protocol: a plain boolean schema bound to the `day` class (self-healed at a fixed id, `hideWhenEmpty` so the panel stays quiet), written from the day page's Properties, read by the calendar grids as a small tint — a skim shows which daily notes you already went over. The flag is an ordinary boolean property — it syncs like everything else.

Filter tabs (All / Daily note / Tasks / Dated / Created) narrow the left column to one section.

## Day pages

A day page is an ordinary page (the journal is the feed of them), and it carries its own chrome:

- **The date header** — the weekday and a Today flag ride a small line above the title; the title itself is the date in your date-format setting, with the day's **ISO week number** as a small flag after it. (The old ‹ / Today / › stepping bar is gone — step days from the calendar popup or the top-bar calendar; the **Reviewed** checkbox lived on that bar and is set from the page's Properties now.)
- **Three sections**, below the page's own content and collapsed like every system section — hidden entirely when there's nothing to show: **Tasks** (open tasks scheduled for this day, overdue above, with the same done checkbox as the Tasks hub), **Dated** (everything else that references the day), and **Created** (objects created that day — main objects only, blocks are inline scaffolding and never listed — newest first, cards by default with an outline switch). **Month and year pages carry the same Created section** — everything created inside that month/year. The day-page branch is read-model only: a node whose id parses at day precision gets the date header and, below its own content, Tasks / Dated / Created — the open-task partition (the tasks hub's exact write on the checkbox), the day node's existing backlink set minus date-chain sources and tasks, and a `createdAt` range query (main nodes only). All three honor the system-sections lazy contract ([links.md](links.md)) — collapsed by default, nothing loads until the expand, hidden entirely when empty (the Created count is the one structured query, gated the same way unlinked mentions gates itself). Embedded renders (the journal feed, the calendar's daily-note embed) skip both: they already sit on aggregation surfaces.

**Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+T** (or the sidebar's **Today** row) opens today's page from anywhere in the app. Note the browser reservation: Ctrl+Shift+T is "reopen closed tab" in most browsers, so the open-today chord fires only where the browser yields it (and as Cmd+Shift+T on macOS). The command palette answers the date affordances — `is_daily:` prefix, Date Pages section, formatted keywords ([search.md](search.md)) — and the CLI verbs are `notees today` / `notees journal`.

## The date model

- **Recurrence is computed, not stored.** A repeating event is one node whose date value carries a small RRULE-lite rule (`daily`, `weekly`, `weekdays`, `monthly`, `yearly` — additive value metadata, SCHEMA.md "Recurrence"; no op, no wire change). Occurrences are virtual: the day view lists the event on every occurrence day (one row per series — never phantom rows), the month grid dots each occurrence, and editing the event edits the series. The rule is authored from the Properties panel's date pill, and a date re-pick keeps it. A repeating task schedules on occurrence days and is never overdue — missed days roll forward to the next occurrence. **Completing an occurrence completes that day, not the series**: the occurrence-done toggle (the tasks hub's checkbox on the day context, the task's own surfaces) records the date in the value's metadata (`completedOccurrences`), the occurrence renders struck-through on its day while every other occurrence stays open, and reopening removes just that date — no nodes are materialized, and the toggle off the occurrence context (the tasks hub) still means "the whole task". Not shipped: the unreviewed-days queue.
- **Events are the calendar's base class; meetings and birthdays are events.** The seeded `event` class is the calendar family root — its single date property (whole-day per the dates law, no clock times) is the date hook the events list reads. `meeting` extends `event` with a meeting-specific family (its own date plus text location/agenda); `birthday` extends `event` the other way — person-typed: a birthday links TO a person (`birthdayPerson`), the date still rides the event chain, and a person's page lists their birthdays through the ordinary backlinks section. Both are ordinary classed pages — queryable, template-able, mentionable; a date dots the month grid and lists the object in the day's Dated section like any dated object, and the event family (event + extenders) lists in the events element on its own once an instance carries a date or lives inside a day page. The system `event`/`meeting`/`birthday` classes are plain seeds; existing workspaces materialize the families through the same idempotent ensure as the task family.
- **Local midnight is the only "today".** Every today path (the shortcut, the calendar, the journal, the default-view landing) derives the day from local midnight; the UTC `.slice` pattern is banned by design.

## Quick capture

**Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+N** quick-adds to today's page or the Inbox from anywhere; at narrow widths a floating **+ button** opens the same quick-add modal ([pages.md](pages.md#narrow-screens)).
